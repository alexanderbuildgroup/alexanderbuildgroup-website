Who we are

This website, alexanderbuildgroup.com, is operated by Alexander Build Group. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to Alexander Build Group.

Information you give us

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email address, phone number, project type, and message. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and to discuss your project.

Our contact form is delivered by Formspree, a form processing service, which passes your message to our business email. If you email or call us directly, we receive the information you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors found the site, the type of device and browser used, and general location (city or region, not exact address). Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. It does not tell us who you are, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record basic technical information, such as IP addresses, for security and operation of its service.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotes
  • To communicate with you about a project
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run this website: Formspree (contact form delivery), Google (analytics and email), and GitHub (website hosting). They process information on our behalf under their own privacy and security practices.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics across websites with Google's browser add-on.
  • You can ask us what personal information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing {{ s.email }}.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry messages for as long as needed to respond and to maintain normal business records, and then delete them.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
